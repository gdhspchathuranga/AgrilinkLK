How AgriLink LK works

Whether you grow or buy, getting started takes a few minutes.

For buyers

  1. Create a buyer accountRegister with your name, phone and district.
  2. Browse and compareSee prices from different farmers for the same produce.
  3. Place your orderOrder in bulk or small quantities with your delivery address.
  4. Track itFollow the order from pending to delivered in your dashboard.

For farmers

  1. Create a farmer accountRegister easily and get your own dashboard.
  2. List your produceAdd photos and set your own price per kilogram.
  3. Manage your stockUpdate quantities so buyers only see what you can supply.
  4. Receive ordersAccept orders from buyers and mark them delivered.

Benefits for farmers

  • Set your own prices per kilogram
