About AgriLink LK

Farmers and buyers, connected directly.

AgriLink LK is a digital platform that connects Sri Lankan farmers directly with buyers, promoting fair pricing and transparent trade.

What we stand for

Three simple ideas guide everything on the platform.

No middlemen

Farmers set their own prices per kilogram and keep more of what their crops are worth.

Verified people

Farmers and buyers are verified, so you know who you are trading with.

Clear details

Every product shows its price, unit and available stock before you order.

For farmers

  • Register and list your produce with photos
  • Set your own prices and manage your stock
  • Receive and manage orders from your dashboard

For buyers

  • Browse fresh local produce and compare prices
  • Order in bulk or in small quantities
  • Follow every order from pending to delivered
